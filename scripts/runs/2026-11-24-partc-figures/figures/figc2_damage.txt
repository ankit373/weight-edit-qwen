Part C damage checks: each blind edit minus the same model's unedited baseline, in points, with 95% paired cluster-bootstrap intervals (10000 draws, seed 20261111; clusters: Global-MMLU sample_id (100), MGSM item (50), IFEval prompt). The grey band is [-2, +2] points and the dashed line is -1 point. Global-MMLU is scored from the logits of A to D; MGSM and IFEval are sampled, seed 1, with 384 and 256 new tokens. ReCoVeR and instruction at end do not touch these prompts (checked on 20 rows per set and model) and are not shown.
